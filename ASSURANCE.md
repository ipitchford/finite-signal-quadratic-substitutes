# Assurance and trust boundary

AI-assisted theoretical research, not externally human peer reviewed or formally verified. Detailed simulated academic judgments and dissent are documented in review-evidence; no institutional REF grade is claimed.

The universal claims rest on complete informal analytic proofs in [paper.tex](paper.tex), rendered in [paper.pdf](paper.pdf), not on finite tests. [CLAIMS.json](CLAIMS.json) records assumptions and exclusions. [PREPARATION_REPLAY.json](PREPARATION_REPLAY.json) records fresh normal and optimized supplied-code executions; these are not independent implementations. The release wrapper already runs the sharp suite, so separate execution is not independent coverage.

The completion checker verifies supplied rational certificates and rejection witnesses. It is not a general exact SOCP solver. Numerical feasibility, tolerance and rounded boundary solutions do not prove an exact certificate. No polynomial bit complexity or rational primal optimum is promised.

[RESEARCH_GATES.json](RESEARCH_GATES.json) provides bounded contribution, source and limitation evidence. Schema completeness is not scientific adequacy or priority clearance. Search silence cannot exclude equivalent minimax-risk or seminorm-extension theorems.

[review-evidence/SYNTHESIS.md](review-evidence/SYNTHESIS.md) and [review-evidence/REVISION_VERIFICATION.md](review-evidence/REVISION_VERIFICATION.md) preserve academic provenance and dissent. The latter reports coordinator compilation and all-page visual inspection; this packaging refresh does not independently repeat PDF layout review. Markdown is a derivative accessibility aid, not a tagged-PDF or screen-reader certification. Remote CI and canonical public readback remain pending.
