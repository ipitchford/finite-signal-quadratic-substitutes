## Checks and remaining limits

The nine citation keys in the manuscript map to bibliography entries; the Markdown formatter retains named citation markers and separate bibliography entries. Bibliographic existence and correct DOI shape alone do not establish contextual support. The current package does not include downloaded third-party source PDFs or pretend that all sources were independently rechecked during packaging.

No equivalent arbitrary-support scalar-knot formulation was located in the passages and searches recorded. This is bounded negative evidence, not proof of originality. Broader minimax-risk, seminorm-extension, information-design and citation-chain searches could reveal an equivalent theorem. No claim of being first, no blanket priority clearance and no desired rating is attached.

Correctness, originality, significance, reproducibility, licensing and public availability are separate dimensions. The final exact-input assessment must be read on its own evidence; earlier review hashes do not certify later bytes.
