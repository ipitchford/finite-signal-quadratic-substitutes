# Sources and their roles

This is a bounded source map, not an exhaustive systematic review. The manuscript bibliography is authoritative; the notes preserve stage-specific search scope. No cited third-party full text is redistributed here.

| Key | Source | Role and limit |
|---|---|---|
| CW | Chen and Waggoner, Informational Substitutes, FOCS 2016 / expanded 2017. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/FOCS.2016.33), [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08636) | Definition 2.2.3 supplies the two-base-signal strong-substitutes framework. Not evidence that this exact completion theorem already appears there. |
| EP | Anonymous, Four tests for binary informational substitutes, candidate v1.1.0. [Repository](https://github.com/ipitchford/binary-quadratic-informational-substitutes), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23238557) | Prior binary-source classification, including sparse support and vector targets; a candidate predecessor, not external peer-review authority. |
| KS | Kong and Schoenebeck, Optimizing Bayesian Information Revelation Strategy in Prediction Markets, ITCS 2018. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2018.14) | Established posterior optimization and support reduction. |
| ACWX | Anunrojwong, Chen, Waggoner and Xu, Computing Equilibria of Prediction Markets via Persuasion, 2020. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03607) | General optimal-garbling algorithms; different optimization target. |
| Thesis | Anunrojwong, Structure and Design of Informational Substitutes, Harvard thesis, 2018. [Institutional record](https://nrs.harvard.edu/urn-3:HUL.InstRepos:38811526) | Mean elicitation, especially Section 2.4; earlier definition's quantifiers must not be conflated with revised CW definition. |
| OP | Ordentlich and Polyanskiy, Strong Data Processing Constant is Achieved by Binary Inputs. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01987) | Established binary reductions for channel divergence contraction. The present coefficient depends on cell-target means, not just the channel. |
| Sion1958 | Sion, On general minimax theorems, Pacific J. Math. 8(1), 171–176. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1958.8.171) | Theorem 3.4 provides classical minimax interchange; not the finite scalar-knot reduction. |
| Lobo1998 | Lobo, Vandenberghe, Boyd and Lebret, Applications of second-order cone programming, LAA 284, 193–228. [Author PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/socp.pdf) | Sections 1.1 and 2.1 provide established conic representation of convex quadratic problems. |
| SMA | Shah, Mandal and Azhar, All Substitution Is Local, 2026. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01443) | Section 2 and Remark 7 concern finite-action piecewise-linear value; distinct from continuous quadratic prediction. |

Further comparison sources, including Börgers et al., Makur–Zheng and Ciosmak's extension geometry, appear with inspected locators in [PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md), [NOVELTY_REFRESH.md](NOVELTY_REFRESH.md) and [SPARSE_EXTENSION.md](SPARSE_EXTENSION.md).
